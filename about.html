Culture & craft

The Craft of Fingerboarding

Fingerboarding began in the late 1980s as crude plastic keychains and homemade wooden decks crafted by skateboarders stuck inside on rainy days. Over four decades, it evolved into a worldwide subculture and specialized engineering artform.

Today's fingerboards mirror full-scale skateboards down to the millimeter: real 5-ply maple decks pressed in molds, miniature aluminum trucks with responsive urethane bushings, and tiny ball-bearing wheels made from skateboard wheel urethane.

Fingerboard Deck Anatomy

Understanding the components that make modern fingerboards perform like real skateboards.

  • 1. 5-Ply Maple DeckPressed maple veneer with a medium concave and kicked nose and tail (typically 32mm to 34mm wide).
  • 2. Foam Grip TapeThin, soft adhesive foam that gives your fingers grip without sandpaper wearing them down.
  • 3. TrucksMiniature trucks with bushings that let the board turn and carve, and give it a realistic pop.
  • 4. Urethane WheelsSmall urethane wheels with micro-bearings so the board rolls smoothly.
